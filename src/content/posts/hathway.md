Hathway designs ordering and loyalty apps for restaurant chains. I was its design systems specialist. I set up our Figma organization from scratch, then moved years of Sketch files, one per client, per project, per redesign, into a single Figma library. Symbols became components with proper architecture, type and color became global styles, and every client's system got a layout anyone could find their way around.

The files were half of it. I brought the whole design team over too: pairing on the new workflow, writing down the conventions, and answering the ping when a component didn't behave. By the end, every active client lived in Figma and every designer worked there daily.

Here's every system, one client at a time.
