Ladle pulls a recipe off any site, strips the life story, and lets you rewrite it: make it vegan, drop the chili flakes, cut the sodium. I did the brand and the app, from first sketches to App Store.

![](https://media.hudbud.net/posts/ladle/render-iphone-2.webp)

## Brand

Four directions, each with its own mark, palette and type. A sun with a ladle hidden in its line. A cubist cut that reveals the ladle in negative space. Two ladle bowls looped into infinity. A Y2K star spun from ladles.
