Osterhout Design Group builds next-generation AR hardware. I contributed branding, marketing materials, UI/UX design with animated interaction prototypes, pixel-perfect icon sets, and web design. Supervised by Britton Holland, ODG's Creative Director.

**Roles:** Branding, Marketing, UI/UX, Web Design

**Tools:** Figma, After Effects, Illustrator

ODG spent six years going from stealth military contractor to the technology spotlight: tech sold to Microsoft in 2014 to power HoloLens, the fastest Android devices on the planet via Qualcomm, CES 2016's mobile device category winner, and a 2018 acquisition by an undisclosed major technology leader.

## ReticleOS

Android doesn't speak dual displays, see-through UI, or world-locking interfaces — so ODG built ReticleOS on top of it: a full application suite, developer support, and Play Store-like services. I designed icon sets, app UI, and animated interaction prototypes for the platform.
