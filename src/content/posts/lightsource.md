A three-year-old procurement platform, nineteen engineers, no designer. I came in first. Eighteen months later it had a brand, a design system, a rebuilt core workflow, and a design team that works the same way.

Legacy, then the redesign. Same page, same data.

## Brand

Fifteen oranges to one. The company asked for a design system and had a PNG logo and a near-empty Notion page for guidelines, so the brand came first: no sense encoding a palette into hundreds of components and changing it later. One #FF4B00, Wasa for display, Inter for everything else, a 27-page book, a new site and every piece of sales material.

## System

Learn one page, learn them all. A small, engineering-first system in Tailwind, cut straight from the brand: one header, one toolbar, one table, on every list page.

## Workflow

*"The platform requires way too many clicks."* A senior buyer at an aerospace manufacturer.

Sourcing projects are where a buyer's day happens: spec, quote, compare, award. The whole thing went up on a wall first, with engineers, for months.
