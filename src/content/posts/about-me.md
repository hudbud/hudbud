I'm a curious, hands-on design leader obsessed with nature, tinkering, and making things with my hands.

Currently, I'm the design lead at [LightSource](https://lightsource.ai). Previously, I built [Carvana](https://www.carvana.com)'s design system, designed apps, websites, and design systems for clients like [Dave & Buster's](https://www.daveandbusters.com), [Denny's](https://www.dennys.com), and [D4](https://bigwalls.net/the-d4-story.html), and worked on bleeding-edge [AR UX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rDWjRJ26ak) back in 2017. I stay busy with too many side projects, plus client work when something excites me.

**Product design** — zero to one, and one to whole. I've launched products from nothing and grown MVPs into cohesive, mature products.

**Design systems** — systems are how craft scales. I built Carvana's design system: tokens, components, docs, and the buy-in that makes a system stick.

**Creative & art direction** — art, technology, innovation: same job. I draw from a wide bench — 3D and animation, photography, machining, circuitry, motion, drawing and painting.

**Branding & spaces** — brands are products too. Fifteen years of studio work: identities, sites, and spaces that live everywhere the product does.

afk: bike builds, fly fishing, climbing, sewing, and riding around SF with Cosmo.
