K Street Pedestrian Passageway Project and Product Logo

The following logos were created for the K Street Pedestrian Passageway project, internally known as the 2Towns passageway project. The project involved developing an interactive Augmented Reality and Lighting experience for people who walk through the K street tunnel. The easiest way to get from old town to new town, the project was appropriately titled 2Towns. The new tunnel aimed to create a more open and welcoming atmosphere for people walking through the once decrepit tunnel.
