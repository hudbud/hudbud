Pieology hired Hathway for an ordering and loyalty app and a web ordering platform. I designed screens, helped design and maintain the design system, and drew the icons for both the app and the site. The UI stays clean and lets the iconography and illustration carry the brand's hand-made streak.

## Design system

The system has four pages. Atoms covers buttons and their states. Core patterns covers color and type. Then two pages of molecules: app and web navigation, then menu cards, category sliders and rewards. The green buttons do the selling.
