Dutch Bros is a coffee chain in the western US. It came to Hathway to improve its loyalty program. I moved the project from Sketch to Figma: every symbol rebuilt as a component with proper architecture, and new global type and color styles. Then I gave the system a clearer layout and kept managing it from there.

## Foundations

Type and color come first. It's Futura PT from the small button text up to the points counter, and every style is global, so one change reaches every screen.

## Atoms, molecules, organisms

The layout builds up from small to large. Atoms are buttons and form fields. Molecules are navigation, tabs and payment. Organisms are the points dials and the reward cards.
