Denny's came to Hathway to redesign ordering on mobile and desktop and to launch a loyalty program that converts. We built a native app, mobile and desktop ordering sites, and a marketing site for both. I designed UI screens across all of it, helped manage the design system, and drew a few illustrations along the way.

## Ordering

Pick a location, browse the menu, check out, and get a pickup window. There's a suggestion or two along the way, then a thank-you that tells you when to show up.
