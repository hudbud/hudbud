Red Robin came to Hathway to make online ordering better. We built three products for it: a mobile ordering app, a mobile ordering site and a desktop ordering site. We used a lot of whitespace and kept the happy path short, to cut down on bounces. I was on the UI team shipping pixel-perfect screens on a tight timeline, and I managed the design system underneath.

## Three surfaces

The app opens on a burger and three promises: order and pay for to-go, order ahead and skip the line, find the nearest Red Robin. The desktop site tells the same story on a bigger screen.
